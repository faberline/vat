# vat docs

Long-form product documents for `vat`. The product contract itself lives at
the repository root: [README.md](../README.md) states the promises,
[STATUS.md](../STATUS.md) the support matrix, and [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md)
the ordered outcomes. Everything under `docs/` explains or expands those three
and must agree with them.

| Area | Index | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| `product/` | [product/README.md](product/README.md) | Architecture of the three pillars: how the native runtime, the shared Linux machine, the Docker Engine socket, persistent K3s, and the machine's local GCP services fit together, and the decided and open spikes. |
